How It Works

Why pasted Urdu names break in Free Fire — and how this converter fixes them.

Urdu letters join

In Urdu, most letters change shape depending on their neighbours. A letter at the start, middle or end of a word takes a different form in each position — that joining is what makes the script flow.

Free Fire draws left-to-right

The game renders nicknames left-to-right and does not shape Urdu script. Paste a normal Urdu name and it appears reversed, with every letter split apart from the next.

We fix the order for you

The converter picks each letter's correct joining form and stores the name in the exact visual order the game draws. Paste the result into Free Fire and it reads perfectly.
